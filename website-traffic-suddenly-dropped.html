Website traffic suddenly dropped?

A sharp drop in visitors from Google feels like a disaster. The first job is to find out whether something on your site broke, or whether Google changed how it ranks pages.

In plain words

What is going on

Drops usually have one of two causes. A technical break: a redesign, a plugin update or a site move removed pages, added a noindex tag, broke redirects or slowed the site. Or a Google change: an algorithm update or new AI answers on the results page now take clicks that used to go to you.

The fix is completely different for each, which is why guessing wastes time. A slow decline over months is usually a third problem: old content losing ground to newer pages.

How to tell

Signs you have this problem

  • Search Console shows clicks falling sharply over a few days, not slowly over months.
  • The drop started right after a website change, migration or new theme.
  • Some pages lost almost everything while others are fine.
  • Pages that used to rank now return 404, redirect to the homepage, or show "noindex".
  • Traffic has been sliding slowly for months on older blog or service pages.

Ticked two or more? Ask for a free audit and we check it properly on your live pages.

Why it matters

Why it costs you money

01

Fewer visitors means fewer calls, bookings and orders, every day the cause is not fixed.

02

Technical breaks often get worse over time as Google drops more pages.

03

Changing the wrong thing (rewriting content when the cause was a redirect) costs money and can make it worse.

In Kuwait and Saudi Arabia

Many Gulf businesses rebuild their website or switch platforms around big seasons such as Ramadan and White Friday. A rebuild without a redirect plan is one of the most common causes of a sudden drop we look for.

FAQ

Questions about this problem

Was it a Google update or did my site break?

That is the first question we answer. If the drop starts right after a site change and only some pages are affected, it is usually technical. If it matches a known update date and many sites in your niche moved, it is more likely Google.

Will my traffic come back?

If the cause was technical and it gets fixed, recovery is common but not guaranteed. If Google changed what it shows, the plan is to adapt the pages rather than wait. We report recovery only when we can measure it.

Do you need my analytics access?

Search Console access gives the best diagnosis. You add us as a user with a limited role. We never need passwords.

How fast can you check?

A first diagnosis normally starts the same week. Tell us the date the drop started when you write to us.
