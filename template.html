FAQ

Questions people ask

What do you actually do?

We are a done-for-you SEO and AEO (AI search) team. We find why customers can't find your business on Google, Google Maps or in AI answers such as ChatGPT, then fix it. SEO and AI-search specialists do the work, and a separate QA reviewer checks every fix on your live site.

What is in the free audit?

We run our tools on your public pages: indexing, broken links and redirects, business facts, schema and AI-crawler access, plus a few real buyer questions asked in AI tools. You get a Fix Queue of up to 15 priorities, ranked P0 (urgent) to P3. It is yours to keep, with no obligation.

Why is my website not showing up on Google?

The usual causes are pages that aren't indexed (because of a 404, a noindex tag, a wrong canonical, thin content or no internal links), redirect problems, or pages that lost rankings over time. The free audit finds the cause for each important URL.

What is AEO, and why does it matter?

AEO (answer engine optimization) means making your business easy for ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to read, trust and name. More people now ask an AI tool for a recommendation instead of searching, and the answer usually names only a few businesses.

Do you work with businesses in Kuwait and Saudi Arabia?

Yes. We are based in Kuwait and work with businesses in the Gulf, the UK, the US, Canada and Australia. We also check your Google Maps facts.

Do you need my passwords?

No, never. The free audit uses your public pages. When we make fixes, you invite us with a limited role (for example editor or manager), and you can remove it at any time.

Do you guarantee rankings or ChatGPT mentions?

No. Nobody controls Google or AI answers, and anyone who guarantees them is not being honest. We guarantee clear evidence: a real audit, a ranked Fix Queue, QA-checked fixes and a recheck.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. We deliver white-label audits and fixes under your agency's brand. Agency prices are on our pricing page, and you can ask for a free sample audit first.
