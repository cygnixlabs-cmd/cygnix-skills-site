Google isn't indexing my pages

A page that Google has not indexed does not exist in search. Nobody can find it, no matter how good it is.

In plain words

What is going on

"Indexing" means Google has read a page and saved it so it can show it in results. Google does not index every page it finds. It skips pages it thinks are duplicates, too thin, blocked, broken, or not important to your own site.

In Google Search Console this shows up as messages like "Crawled - currently not indexed", "Discovered - currently not indexed", "Excluded by noindex tag" or "Alternate page with proper canonical tag". Each one has a different cause, and the fix depends on the cause.

How to tell

Signs you have this problem

  • Searching site:yourdomain.com on Google shows fewer pages than your site really has.
  • Search Console lists pages as "Crawled - currently not indexed" or "Discovered - currently not indexed".
  • A new service or product page has been live for weeks and still cannot be found.
  • Nothing on your site links to the page (an "orphan" page).
  • The page has a noindex tag or points its canonical to another page by mistake.

Ticked two or more? Ask for a free audit and we check it properly on your live pages.

Why it matters

Why it costs you money

01

Your most important pages (services, products, locations) can't bring in a single visitor from Google.

02

Money spent on writing or designing those pages is wasted until they are indexed.

03

If Google skips many pages, it can take your whole site less seriously.

In Kuwait and Saudi Arabia

Bilingual sites in Kuwait and Saudi Arabia often have an Arabic and an English version of each page. If the two versions are not linked correctly (hreflang and canonical tags), Google may index only one of them, or neither. We check both versions.

FAQ

Questions about this problem

What does "Crawled - currently not indexed" mean?

Google visited the page but decided not to add it to its index yet. Common reasons are thin or duplicate content, weak internal links, or the page looking unimportant compared with the rest of your site.

Can I just click "Request indexing"?

You can, and sometimes it works. If the underlying cause is still there, Google often drops the page again. Fix the cause first, then request indexing.

Do you need my Search Console password?

No. We never ask for passwords. You add us as a user in Search Console with a limited role, and you can remove us at any time.

How long does indexing take after a fix?

Often days to a few weeks, and Google decides. We recheck after the fixes and tell you which URLs are indexed and which are still waiting.
