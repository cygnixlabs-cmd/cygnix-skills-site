Salon or spa not getting found locally?

Salon and spa customers decide fast, usually from the map, the photos and the reviews they see in the first few seconds. If you are not on that screen, you are not considered.

In plain words

What is going on

Local search runs on three things: your Google Business Profile, your website, and the facts and reviews other sites hold about you. When a customer searches "nail salon in [area]" or asks ChatGPT for a recommendation, Google and AI tools pick businesses whose services, area and hours are clear and match everywhere.

Many salons rely on Instagram alone. Instagram is great for showing work, but search engines and AI tools read it far less than a website and a complete Google listing.

How to tell

Signs you have this problem

  • Searching your service plus your area does not show you on the map or the first page.
  • Your services and prices are only in Instagram posts or a picture of a price list.
  • Your opening hours or phone differ between Google, Instagram and your website.
  • You have no website page for your main services (for example bridal, keratin, massage).
  • AI tools name other salons near you but not yours.

Ticked two or more? Ask for a free audit and we check it properly on your live pages.

In Kuwait and Saudi Arabia

In Kuwait and Saudi Arabia many salons and spas book mainly through Instagram and WhatsApp. That works for existing customers, but new customers searching Google Maps or asking an AI tool need a listing and a simple website with the same facts.

FAQ

Questions about this problem

I get customers from Instagram. Do I still need this?

Instagram keeps existing customers engaged. New customers who search Google or ask an AI tool mostly see Google listings and websites, so those need to be complete too.

Do I need a big website?

No. A clear page for each main service, your area, hours, booking method and real photos is often enough.

Can you get us more reviews?

We do not buy or write reviews, ever. We can make it easier for happy customers to leave one, using methods Google allows.

Can you guarantee a top position?

No. Map results depend on distance, relevance and reviews, and Google decides. We fix what is in your control and show the before and after.
