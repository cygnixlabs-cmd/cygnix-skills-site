In plain words

What is going on

AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews write an answer by reading websites, reviews and business listings, then naming the businesses they trust most. They can only name you if they can read your site, understand exactly what you do and where, and find the same facts about you in more than one place.

Most small business websites were built only for people looking at a screen. The important facts (services, prices, area, opening hours) sit inside images, sliders or vague text, or AI crawlers are blocked by accident. So the AI tool picks someone easier to understand.

How to tell

Signs you have this problem

  • You ask ChatGPT or Gemini "best [your service] in [your city]" and your business is not named.
  • The AI names you but gets your hours, prices, phone or location wrong.
  • Your site has no clear page for each service, or the services are only listed in a picture.
  • Your robots.txt or security plugin blocks bots such as GPTBot, PerplexityBot or Google-Extended.
  • Your site has no structured data (schema) describing your business.

Ticked two or more? Ask for a free audit and we check it properly on your live pages.

Why it matters

Why it costs you money

01

Every buyer question answered without you is a lead that goes straight to a competitor, before they ever search Google.

02

Wrong facts in an AI answer (old prices, closed branch, wrong phone) lose customers who do try to reach you.

03

In Kuwait and Saudi Arabia

In Kuwait and Saudi Arabia many people ask in both English and Arabic, and AI tools often rely on Google Maps listings, directories and Instagram bios for local facts. Few local businesses have prepared for AI answers yet, so a clear, consistent site can stand out quickly.

FAQ

Questions about this problem

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No. Nobody controls what AI tools say, and anyone who guarantees it is not being honest. What we can do is remove the reasons AI tools skip you (blocked crawlers, unclear pages, conflicting facts) and re-run the same buyer questions to show what changed.

What is AEO or GEO?

AEO (answer engine optimization) and GEO (generative engine optimization) are names for the same idea: making your website easy for AI assistants to read, trust and quote. It builds on normal SEO; it does not replace it.

Do I need an llms.txt file?

It is a small text file that tells AI tools what your site is about and which pages matter. It helps, it is quick to add, and it is one of the items our audit checks. On its own it will not make you appear in answers.

How long before AI answers change?

It varies by tool. Some read the web live, others update less often. We re-run the same fixed set of buyer questions after the fixes so you can see the difference for yourself rather than trusting a promise.
