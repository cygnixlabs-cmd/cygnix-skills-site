Pricing FAQ

Questions about packages

What is a white-label AI visibility audit?

It is an SEO + AEO audit of one client site, delivered with your agency's logo and no Cygnix branding. It shows how the site looks to Google and to AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, and lists the fixes in priority order.

How much does it cost?

$99 for one client site, or $299 for a pack of 4 audits ($74.75 each). Monthly plans start at $29 a month.

How do I pay?

There is no card checkout on this site. Book a package, we confirm the scope by email, and we send an invoice. The payment options are listed on the invoice.

Can I see a sample before buying?

Yes. Request a free sample audit for one site and we email you the real report, so you can check the format before you buy.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No one can honestly guarantee rankings. We guarantee clear evidence: a report from a real tool run, a prioritised fix list, and a recheck after the fixes.
