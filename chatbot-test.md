## How it works

- Knowledge base: `assets/kb.json`, built by `build_kb.py` (run from `build.py`) from `feed/cygnix-listings.json`, `feed/apify-actors.json` (public Apify store data), the packages in `build.py` and the FAQs in both templates. 17 actors, 69 skills + 1 bundle, 8 packages, FAQs and ~30 curated topics.
- Matching: client-side keyword + synonym scoring with IDF weights, light stemming and typo tolerance (one edit or a swapped pair). No paid API, no server.
- Low confidence or unknown words: asks a clarifying question, shows up to 3 suggestions, and offers a free 15-min consultation, email to info@cygnixlabs.com, or sending the chat to the team.
- Session memory: sessionStorage keeps the conversation, the visitor type (agency, local business, store, creator) and the last topic, so follow-ups like "How much?" work.
